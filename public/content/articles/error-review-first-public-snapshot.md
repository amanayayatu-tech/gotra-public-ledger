# Error Review: What The First Snapshot Does Not Prove

Boundary: Research information only. Not investment advice. Not a trading signal. No performance proof. No guarantee of future performance.

The first public snapshot is useful because it shows the error surface instead of hiding it. It is still limited:

- Local checks are not formal acceptance.
- A resolved record such as [PRED-20260203-TSM-0054](#/ledger/PRED-20260203-TSM-0054) is one row, not a broad conclusion.
- Pending and frozen-pending records remain unresolved on [Ledger](#/ledger).
- Blocked or review-needed outcome states are honest data-boundary outputs, not hidden failures or successes.

The correct next question is whether the resolver, manifest, and paper portfolio rules remain deterministic and public-safe. See [Methodology](#/methodology), [Sources](#/sources), and [Performance](#/performance) for those boundaries.
